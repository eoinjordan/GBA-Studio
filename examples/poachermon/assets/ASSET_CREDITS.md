# Poachermon showcase asset credits

The custom Poachermon backgrounds and sprites bundled with this sample are original work released into the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

They include the 240x160 Reedline Ranger Outpost, the field-ranger player, Captain Rowan, Finn, Ash, Moss, the evidence snare, and the rescued forest creature. The shipping files were authored specifically for the GBA engine's 8x8 background tiles, four-color sprite palettes, explicit palette slots, and alpha transparency.

The remaining UI, emote, and font assets are inherited from the standard GB Studio template and retain their original licensing.
