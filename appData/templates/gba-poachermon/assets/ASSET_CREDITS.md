# Poachermon showcase asset credits

The production environment is derived from Kenney's
[Roguelike/RPG Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-rpg-pack), and the
character silhouettes are derived from the
[CC0 Man Sprite](https://opengameart.org/content/man-sprite-16x16) by dutzy.
Both sources are released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The assets are cropped, recomposed, recolored, and palette-reduced for the GBA
engine. The resulting 240x160 ranger outpost and all character, evidence, and
creature sprites remain CC0.

The remaining UI, emote, and font assets are inherited from the standard GB Studio template and retain their original licensing.
